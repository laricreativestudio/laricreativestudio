The Story

Approach

An ongoing portrait series feeding a brand's monthly social content system. The brief called for a considered, editorial treatment — every choice of location, framing and pacing was made in service of the story, not just to fill a page.

Placeholder photography stands in for the finished set below; each frame is labelled so real photography can be dropped in without touching the layout.
