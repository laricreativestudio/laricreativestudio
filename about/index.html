About

About Lari Creative Studio

Creative thinking. Visual storytelling. Digital possibility.

Who We Are

A multidisciplinary creative, digital and technology studio

Lari Creative Studio is a full-service creative agency based in Lagos, working with startups, growing companies, corporations and government agencies across Nigeria, West Africa and internationally.

We combine strategy, design, technology and storytelling across ten service lines — brand identity, web design, UI/UX, photography, videography, social content, digital marketing, business consulting, AI automation and training. Photography and visual storytelling are one part of a broader creative capability, not the whole of it.

Studio team at work
Studio positioning — strategy session
Market Position

A strategic partner, not a low-cost vendor

We compete on value, professionalism and full-service capability rather than price alone — serving freelancers and churches at the Essential level, up to banks, universities and government agencies at the Enterprise level, without diluting our positioning.

Key Differentiators

  • — Full-service capability under one roof, reducing the need for clients to coordinate multiple vendors.
  • — Transparent, tiered pricing that simplifies client decision-making at every stage of growth.
  • — Early investment in AI automation capability, ahead of many local competitors.
  • — A phased growth model built around recurring revenue, not just one-off projects.
Design process — sketches and reviews
Our Philosophy

A logo isn't a brand.

Great design should grow businesses, not just decorate them. We tie every creative decision — a colour, a layout, a shot — back to a business outcome, and we say so plainly rather than dressing it up in jargon.

We sound like a senior creative partner advising a client: confident without arrogance, clear without hype.

Our Values

What we hold ourselves to

Craft over decoration

Every deliverable is held to a professional standard, not just a passable one.

Clarity over jargon

We explain technical decisions in plain language, and tie creative work to outcomes.

Consistency over one-offs

Our tiered structure and documented process mean quality doesn't depend on which day you called.

Growth over vanity

We measure success in enquiries, conversions and retained clients — not just how something looks.

Our Roadmap

The five-year plan

Year 1

Build the Foundation

Deliver consistent quality across core creative services, build a strong portfolio, and establish SOPs, proposal templates and a referral pipeline.

Year 2

Expand the Offering

Launch digital marketing and business & brand consulting as standalone offerings, and introduce monthly retainer contracts.

Year 3

Build Recurring Revenue

Grow the retainer client base as a share of total revenue, and begin piloting AI Automation Essential packages with early-adopter clients.

Year 4

Scale AI & Technology

Formalise AI Automation Solutions across all three tiers and build dedicated CRM/ERP integration capability.

Year 5

Build the Ecosystem

Launch Lari Academy at scale — bootcamps, online courses and mentorship — and diversify revenue across creative services, AI and education.

A strategic planning tool, not a public commitment — the milestones we're building toward as the studio grows from a project-based practice into a full, education-inclusive ecosystem.

Founder & Creative Director

Oluwatimilehin Monilari

Oluwatimilehin founded Lari Creative Studio to bring brand strategy, design and technology under one roof, so clients get one accountable team instead of a folder of disconnected vendors. That founder-led structure is why every engagement — from a first logo to an Enterprise AI build — is still held to the same standard of craft.

Why Clients Work With Us

Full-service capability, transparent pricing

One team accountable for the whole chain — strategy, identity, web, content, marketing and increasingly, AI — under a transparent, tiered pricing structure that scales from a first logo to full digital transformation.
