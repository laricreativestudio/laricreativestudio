Why it matters

Why brand identity & logo design matters

A considered identity is the first thing a client, partner or lender judges you by. It sets the ceiling for how seriously your business is taken before anyone reads a word.

Ideal for

  • — Startups building a first identity
  • — Businesses overdue for a rebrand
  • — Organisations that need a documented brand system

Our Process

How we deliver brand identity & logo design

01

Discovery

A structured questionnaire and (from Professional) a strategy session to understand audience, positioning and competitors.

02

Concepts

Multiple logo directions explored, narrowed to the strongest concept.

03

Refinement

Revision rounds to sharpen the mark, palette and type pairing.

04

Guidelines

A brand guide documenting correct usage — from a basic one-pager to a full enterprise system.
